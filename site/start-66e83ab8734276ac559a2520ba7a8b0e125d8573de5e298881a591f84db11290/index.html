When the task is harder

Give Copilot more time to think

Auto is a good everyday choice. For a complicated plan, comparison or difficult question, open the menu shown and choose Think Deeper.

  1. 1Select Auto at the top of a new chat.
  2. 2Choose Think Deeper or the GPT Think Deeper option available to you.
  3. 3Expect it to take a little longer. More thinking can help, but you still need to check the answer.

What is a Page?

A Page turns a Copilot answer into an editable workspace. Use it when you want to keep shaping an outline, plan or draft beside the chat. Look for Edit in Pages below a response. Changes save automatically.

Before you share anything

Never use an AI answer unchecked.

AI can be helpful and still be wrong—just like a person doing research can make a mistake. You are responsible for what you put in and what you take out.

1

Read it properly

Does it make sense? Does it answer your actual question? Is anything important missing?

2

Check the risky details

Verify names, dates, numbers, links, quotations, legal points and anything that affects a decision.

3

Use a reliable source

Look at the original document or official website. A quick web search can help, but prefer the source itself.

4

Ask for a challenge

Start a new chat and ask it to find unsupported claims, errors and gaps. Another AI can offer a second view—not proof.

Try this in a fresh chat

“Review the text below. List every factual claim that needs checking, say why, and suggest a reliable original source. Do not assume the text is correct.”

Our AI policy

Five golden rules of AI

These rules apply whichever AI tool you use. Microsoft 365 Copilot is our preferred tool, but stronger protection does not remove your responsibility to use it safely.

Read the full AI Usage Policy ↗Visit the AI SharePoint page ↗
1

Protect information

Do not enter personal, sensitive or confidential information. If it concerns beneficiaries, staff, donors, safeguarding, finance, contracts or internal documents, do not paste it.

2

You own the output

Always review, fact-check, edit and approve AI-generated content before using or sharing it.

3

Do not connect unapproved tools

No browser plug-ins, automations or external meeting assistants without IT approval.

4

No AI photos or videos of our work

Charts and diagrams are fine. Do not create images or videos representing projects or communities.

5

When in doubt, ask

If you are unsure whether something is appropriate, check with your manager or IT first.

What does a safe starting point look like?

Removing a person’s name is not always enough to make information anonymous.

✓ Safe starting points

  • General task lists with no names or private detail
  • Rough notes that are genuinely non-sensitive
  • Public information and general background research
  • Ideas, outlines, templates and fictional examples
  • Fully anonymised content that cannot be linked back to a person

× Never enter

  • Personal data about staff, participants, donors or partners
  • Health, safeguarding, vulnerability or financial information
  • Confidential emails, legal matters or case information
  • Anything that could identify someone from the context
  • Organisational data in a tool that may train on it
How do I know which AI tool I can use?+

Use Microsoft Copilot Chat where it is available. It works within our Microsoft 365 security, identity and data protection controls, so it is preferable to consumer or free AI tools.

Other AI tools may be used for low-risk work such as general drafting, brainstorming and improving non-sensitive material, provided you follow all five golden rules. Check that available settings prevent your inputs being used for model training or service improvement. If the data handling is unclear, use Copilot or ask IT.

Do not use AI for high-risk work involving safeguarding, financial or eligibility decisions, contracts, legal interpretation or performance decisions affecting people.
